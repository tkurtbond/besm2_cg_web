## Before you start

This guide takes you through a character on this page a step at a
time. It can stay open while you work: **Next** and **Back** move
between the steps, and **Steps** lists them all, so you can jump to
any of them.

Every name shown with its pages has a **?** that opens the rulebook
there: the first number is the page printed in the book, the second
the PDF's. The page can't find your copy of the PDF by itself, so the
first **?** asks you to choose it, and **Rulebook > Choose PDF...**
picks another one later.
[Show me](show:choose-pdf)

A **Show me** button, like the one above, brings up the control a
step is talking about and marks it for a moment.

For the whole of the process in one place, see
[the summary](page:CreationSummaryPage).

## Talk with your GM

Before any numbers, ask your Game Master what kind of game it will
be, and what the campaign allows:
[GM Discussion](page:GmDiscussionPage).

The answers go in **Character > Creation...**: the
**Character Points** you have to spend, the **Background Points** on
offer, the genre that prices the Skills, **The GM's Skill
Points**, and the optional rules and ceilings your GM uses. **OK**
keeps what you set, and **Cancel** puts back what was there.
[Show me](show:creation)

If your GM hands you a campaign file, **File > New from Campaign...**
reads it, and every new character starts with its settings.
[Show me](show:new-from-campaign)

## Who is the character?

Begin with an idea rather than with numbers:
[Character Outline](page:CharacterOutlinePage). A rough one will do.

On the **Character** tab, type the **Name** and a **Tagline**, a few
words that sum the character up.
[Show me](show:name)

**Size** is plain text, for whatever your group notes there; the
page keeps it and prints it, and doesn't price it.
[Show me](show:size)

## Body, Mind and Soul

The three Stats come next: [Assign Stats](page:StatsPage).

Set each one's value with its spinner or by typing it. The **Points**
column beside it says what it costs; if your GM uses the
[staged costs](page:StagedStatCostPage), turn them on in
**Character > Creation...** and the column follows.
[Show me](show:stats)

## Attributes

Attributes are the character's special abilities, powers and
advantages: [Character Attributes](page:AttributesPage), and
[the kinds there are](page:AttributeTypesPage).

1. On the **Attributes** tab, choose a name under the list. Each name
   shows its pages, and **?** opens the rulebook at them.
2. Set its **Level**.
3. Type its **subject**, if it has one, and any **details**.
4. If it has options that change its cost, they are listed under it:
   choose them before you add it.
5. Press **Add**.

To change one, click its line, change what you need, and press
**Change**; **Remove** takes it off the list.
[Show me](show:attributes)

## Weapons

A [](item:Weapon Attack) has more to it than a level. Add it as any
Attribute, then click its line and press **Weapon...**.
[Show me](show:weapon)

The dialog sets its own **Level** and its
[abilities](page:WeaponAbilitiesPage) and
[disabilities](page:WeaponDisabilitiesPage), and shows the damage
and the points that come of them. **OK** keeps it all, and **Cancel**
leaves the attack as it was.

## Defects

Defects are the character's troubles, and each one gives back
Bonus Points: [Character Defects](page:DefectsPage).

On the **Defects** tab, choose a name, set its **rank**, type the
**subject** and **details** if it needs them, and press **Add**, just
as for an Attribute.
[Show me](show:defects)

## Skills

Skills are what the character has learned:
[Skill System](page:SkillsPage).

On the **Skills** tab, choose a Skill and its **Level**, and type
its **specialisations**, separated by commas. The **genre** below
sets what each Skill costs; it is the campaign's, from
**Character > Creation...**.
[Show me](show:skills)

## The derived values

The combat values, Health Points, Energy Points and the rest follow
from the Stats and Attributes: [Derived Values](page:DerivedValuesPage).
Your GM may also use [Shock Value](page:ShockValuePage).

The page works each one out and shows it. To use a number of your
own, type it in the box under it; empty the box to go back to the
page's.
[Show me](show:derived)

## Background

The last step rewards writing about the character:
[Background Points](page:BackgroundPointsPage).

On the **Background** tab, write the **Description**, and keep short
**Notes**: type one, and press **Add**. How many
**Background Points** the writing earns is the GM's call, set in
**Character > Creation...**.
[Show me](show:description)

## Check the points

The Character tab's **Points** lines add it all up: what the Stats
and Attributes cost, what the Defects give back, and what is left of
the Character Points and the Skill Points. A line over its limit turns
red.
[Show me](show:points)

**Warnings** lists whatever the page finds against the rules or the
campaign's settings, such as a Stat over its ceiling. The other tabs
with a list show them too, below it.
[Show me](show:warnings)

## A mecha

A character who owns a mecha takes [](item:Own a Big Mecha), whose
subject names the mecha. The **Mecha** tab lists each one, with the
Mecha Points it gives and what the mecha spends of them.
[Show me](show:mecha)

**New Mecha** makes the mecha a line names, adds it to the file, and
shows it; **Show Mecha** goes to one that is already there.
**Character > Add Mecha** adds one without an owner.
[Show me](show:new-mecha)

A mecha is built on the same tabs as a character, with the
[mecha's own Attributes](page:MechaAttributesPage) and
[Defects](page:MechaDefectsPage) at the end of their lists. The
choice at the right of the menu bar shows any character or mecha
in the file.
[Show me](show:entities)

## Save and print

**File > Save** downloads the file, which you open again later with
**File > Open...**, or by dropping it on the page.
[Show me](show:save)

The **Preview** tab shows the file as it prints, in the
**Format:** you choose. **Copy** puts that text on the clipboard,
and **Save...** downloads it.
[Show me](show:preview)

## Later on

As the game goes on, the character grows:
[Character Advancement](page:AdvancementPage). Open the file again,
make the changes your GM allows, and save it.
